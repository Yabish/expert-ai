# expert-ai

An open-source, self-hostable AI data analyst. It connects read-only to your databases, learns what your data means, and answers questions in English or Arabic with safe queries, charts and recommendations. You bring the AI provider: cloud, Chinese or fully local.

> 🚧 **Under construction.** Nothing here is usable yet. See [`docs/SPEC.md`](docs/SPEC.md) for what we are building and [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) for how we work.

## Why AGPL

expert-ai is licensed under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). You can use, self-host, modify and redistribute it freely. If you run a modified version as a network service for other people, you must share your modifications under the same license. We want everyone who improves the analyst to give those improvements back, including hosted SaaS offerings, where a permissive license or the plain GPL would not require it. Using expert-ai inside your organization to analyze your own data creates no obligation on that data or on your other systems.
